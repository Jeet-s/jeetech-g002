Flow Forge: Pipe Puzzle Game

Rotate pipes and route every fluid home: 30 handcrafted offline logic puzzles.

Get it on Google Play

Logic puzzles offline you can play anywhere, featuring precise pipe routing and tile rotation.

Build working paths by rotating straight and elbow pipes to guide fluids into matching drains. Every move is deliberate: some pipes are bolted, some are decoys, and splitters can feed several drains—use careful logic to earn stars and coins.

What’s inside
• 30 levels across 5 chapters: Workshop Basics (1–6), Factory Floor (7–12), Pressure Plant (13–18), Color Lab (19–24), Refinery Riddles (25–30)
• Mechanics evolve as you progress: rotate pipes, handle drain mouths and several drains, avoid decoy pipes, manage bolted pipes, use splitter tees, route two fluids, navigate cross-overpass “+”, and master three fluids
• Star rating per level based on your solution quality; replay anytime to improve
• Helpful tools: hints, undo, and restart are available when you need them
• Sound and haptics settings let you tune feedback to your preference
• Plays fully offline; progress is stored on your device only
